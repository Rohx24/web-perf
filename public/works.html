06Android · Navigation

DriveDash

A navigation app for an always-on car tablet, built from scratch in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Turn-by-turn directions with live traffic-aware routing that reroutes within about 3 s of leaving the route, a heading-up camera with speed-adaptive zoom, a windshield HUD mode that mirrors the map for reflection onto glass, Spotify controls kept alive by a session watchdog, and an iPhone GPS relay that feeds a Kalman filter when the tablet's own GPS is weak.
