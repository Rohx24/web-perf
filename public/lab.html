02Featured buildAndroid · Kotlin

A car dashboard, from the map up.

Illustration, not a screen recording

DriveDash

Navigation · Music · HUD · for Android tablets

I wanted an always-on screen in the car that did the three things I actually use while driving, and did them well. So I built one from scratch in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose: turn-by-turn navigation with live traffic, Spotify on the same screen, and a HUD mode you can reflect onto the windshield at night.

Navigation

  • Turn-by-turn on Google Routes API v2
  • Traffic-coloured route, green to red
  • Reroutes about 3 s after you leave the route
  • Driven road trims away behind the puck

Map & camera

  • Heading-up camera that turns with you
  • Rate-limited bearing, no spinning on sharp turns
  • Zooms out on highways, in on local streets
  • Place search with autocomplete

HUD

  • Mirrors the map to reflect onto glass
  • Cyan on black for low-light driving
  • Full navigation puck in the HUD mini-map

Music & GPS relay

  • Spotify art and controls, with a session watchdog
  • iPhone GPS relayed over Wi-Fi to a built-in HTTP server
  • Kalman filter to steady a noisy fix
  1. Faster rerouting. Off-route triggers in 3 s instead of 5 s, and the puck lets go of the old route at once.
  2. Path trimming. Crossed segments disappear behind the puck in real time.
  3. Camera stability. Bearing smoothing with hysteresis ended the uncontrolled spinning.
  4. Spotify reliability. A watchdog and exponential backoff stopped sessions dropping after five or six songs.
